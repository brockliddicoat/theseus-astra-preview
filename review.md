## What changed

A new story replaces the 131-scene baseline with denser problem → mechanism → correction → benefit explanations. Theseus is blue; comparison yellow is sparse; type and stage remain neutral. Drawings, motion, textures and 3D geometry are code-based. The six-view inspector includes the authored scan-derived shell, a coating/etched-composite concept, GT01-style switch, wheel, and final plan-5 board A/B geometry.

The revised board uses true 1 mm fillets and equal finished side/rear notch straights of 12.054261463 mm. Its shared JSON drives both views. The weight allowance is computed from one parts file. The 2D/3D GT01 profiles now use matching revised housing coordinates. These are explanatory design models, not manufacturing releases.

## Review without losing feedback

Reviews and text edits are browser-local. Export a JSON review to share it or move it to another device. Import retains prior local data; a reopened clause stays open across imports and build updates. Original notes, original scores, clause responses and later project rules remain searchable. Implementation status is separate from user acceptance.

Use Zoom diagram for a full-resolution pannable view on a phone. The portrait caption remains readable below the canvas. Open 3D parts to rotate or explode a part. Cover and closing scenes remain typography-led.
